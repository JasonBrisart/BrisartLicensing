# Brisart Licensing

The central source of truth for licensing, subscriptions, commercial-use rights, and ecosystem participation across the Brisart software and framework ecosystem.

## Covered Projects

This repository governs licensing and participation for:

- BrisartResearchArchive
- BrisartDevTools
- BrisartPreservationTools
- BrisartIdentityTools
- BrisartFrameworks
- Any future Brisart project unless that project explicitly states otherwise.

## What's In This Repository

### LICENSE.md

The only legally controlling document.

Covers:

- Free-use rights
- Subscription rights
- Commercial-use rights
- Framework use rights
- Cancellation terms
- Pricing
- Definitions and ecosystem participation requirements

### PARTICIPATION.md

Non-binding guidance on ecosystem participation.

Covers:

- Donations
- Implementation requests
- Research participation

Does not grant, modify, or restrict any legal right. Refer to `LICENSE.md` for all permissions, restrictions, and subscription rights.

## Frameworks

Brisart Frameworks are original theories, models, methodologies, specifications, workflows, symbolic structures, technical frameworks, research frameworks, and related structured materials developed and published by Brisart.

Unless otherwise stated, Brisart Frameworks may reference this repository for licensing terms, subscription rights, commercial-use rights, participation policies, and related ecosystem requirements.

## Philosophy

Brisart software and frameworks are developed first to support the operational and research needs of the Brisart ecosystem itself.

The ecosystem emphasizes:

- Local-first operation
- Auditability
- Long-term maintainability
- Source availability
- Research-oriented development

Researchers, labs, institutions, and organizations with similar requirements may benefit from the same ongoing development, subject to the terms described in `LICENSE.md`.
